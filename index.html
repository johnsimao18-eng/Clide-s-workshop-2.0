Welcome to Clide's workshop 2.0

At our mechanic shop, we specialize in delivering high-performance upgrades and precision repairs for all types of vehicles. From expertly installing and tuning coilover suspension systems for improved handling, to engine diagnostics, rebuilds, and performance enhancements, we ensure your car runs at its best. We also offer custom exhaust system fabrication and upgrades to boost both power and sound, giving your ride a unique edge. Whether you’re after everyday reliability or track-ready performance, we combine skill, quality parts, and attention to detail to keep you moving with confidence.

Our workshop is more than just a place to fix cars — it’s where passion meets precision. Every vehicle that enters our doors is treated with the same care and dedication as if it were our own. With state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and a team of skilled, certified mechanics, we turn challenges into solutions and breakdowns into fresh beginnings. From routine servicing to complex repairs, we work with integrity, transparency, and a commitment to excellence. Our mission is to keep you moving forward with confidence, knowing your journey is supported by people who truly care.
